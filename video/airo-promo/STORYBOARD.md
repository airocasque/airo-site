## Message & tone
- **Sentence:** I want to say "un casque propre, désinfecté et sec en 10 minutes" in a premium tone, so the viewer feels that the problem is finally handled, without effort.
- **Tone arc:** urgent → bold → trustworthy → premium → warm
- accent: AIRO blue #2f6bff (stage navy, one blue glow per frame; the glow turns white only when the helmet is ready)
- **Motif:** the spotlight cone — it clicks on for the reveal and returns, wider, on the CTA.

## The seven questions
1. Say « propre, désinfecté, sec en 10 min » in a premium tone: dark keynote stage, one light per frame.
2. Repeats: none in a row; the spotlight is a declared motif (2 appearances, wider the second time).
3. Transitions: hold-frame = « wait for it »; push-through = we enter the machine; motion match = the door closing becomes the dial starting; focal iris = we zoom into the result; colour match = the « ready » blue becomes the brand.
4. Colour: navy with no accent in the hook; blue appears with the borne (colour event 1); turns white-hot when the helmet is ready (colour event 2).
5. New component: **steam manometer** — a pressure-gauge arc whose needle is a plume of steam; it fills 0 → 10 min, and the condensation on its glass clears as it reaches 100 %.
6. Surprises: silence + light click at 0:01.8; focus pull + micro-silence at ~0:10.8.
7. First film in the ledger history.
